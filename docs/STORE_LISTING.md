**Name:** XennDo

**Short description:** Offline tasks, daily focus, lists and reminders in a calm planner.

**Description:**

Organize your tasks and plan the day with XennDo, an offline task planner for
Android. No account is needed.

- Review today's deadlines and overdue work.
- Choose three active focus tasks and keep completed selections visible.
- Browse upcoming dates without losing sight of later tasks.
- Organize custom lists, priorities, notes and subtasks.
- Find work with search and combined filters.
- Review completion history and progress based on your real task records.
- Set optional local reminders and choose System, Light or Dark appearance.
- Export a readable backup and restore it with an explicit replace-all preview.

Your work stays in private local app storage unless you choose to export it.
Backups are not encrypted. Reminder delivery depends on Android permissions
and battery restrictions; alarms can be delayed.
